## What Fire Academy is

Fire Academy is a training club's complete online presence: a public site that showcases the offer **and** an enrollment system that actually handles participant sign-ups, monthly billing, and email communication — no spreadsheets, no manual coordination, no third-party forms.

A single application plays three roles:

- **Customer-facing site** — browse the offer, see session details, get to know the staff, and sign up for a specific date in seconds.
- **Organizer panel** — manage the entire offer, all enrollments, and the monthly accounts of recurring trainings from one place, with automatic participant notifications.
- **Coach's workspace** — a private training plan kept together with an individual client: a shared calendar, goals, body weight, and a comment thread on every entry.

This is not a static landing page. Every date has its own schedule, price, and roster of registered participants; every recurring training has a monthly bill that reacts to days off and cancelled sessions; and the system handles enrollment rules, deadlines, and data-privacy compliance on its own.

## Who it's for

| Audience | What they get |
|----------|---------------|
| **Participant** | A quick overview of the offer and self-service sign-up from a personal account, with an email confirmation. |
| **Regular attendee** | A weekly slot booked as a monthly subscription, with the current month's bill, next month's estimate, and refunds visible in the account. |
| **1-on-1 client** | A private plan from the coach: a shared calendar, ticking sessions off with an effort rating, morning weight, goals, and a comment thread. |

## The offer — three pillars

The whole application is organized around three session categories, each with its own page, staff, and schedule:

- 🥊 **Trainings** — personal and small-group sessions.
- 🏕️ **Camps** — residential sports camps.
- 🎓 **Courses** — including coaching certifications.

Every category describes **what** it offers through **types** — a given kind of session, with a description and a photo gallery. They differ in **when**, because a camp and a weekly training are not booked the same way:

- **Camps and courses run on dates** — a concrete "when and for how much": date, hours, location, price, and open enrollment. A date is booked once.
- **Trainings run on recurring slots** — a fixed weekday and hour with an assigned coach, a cap on participants, and a price per session. A slot is not booked once; it is **subscribed to, month after month**.

## How it works

### The participant's path — camps and courses

1. Opens the site and picks a category (Camps / Courses).
2. Browses the **types** of sessions (gallery, description) and the upcoming **dates**.
3. For each date, sees the price and location at a glance.
4. **Creates an account or logs in** — by email and password or with Google. Sign-up requires an account; a guest who clicks "Sign up" is redirected to the login page and returned to the date afterwards.
5. Signs up in one click — personal data is taken from the profile, with an optional note for the organizer.
6. Receives a **confirmation email**, while the organizer gets a notification about the new sign-up. Bookings can be reviewed and cancelled from the account ("My bookings").

The system enforces the rules on its own: it prevents signing up twice for the same date and **automatically closes enrollment 24 hours before the start** (directing people to contact the club directly).

### Recurring trainings — the monthly subscription

Regular trainings do not work as one-off dates. The Trainings page lists the club's **weekly slots grouped by day of the week**, with a month switcher; each slot shows its coach, the price per session, the remaining spots, and which sessions are already cancelled or fall on a day off that month.

1. Signing up for a slot from an account opens a **monthly subscription**, not a single booking. Before confirming, the dialog shows **the amount for the chosen starting month** — and, if the person already attends other trainings, their combined total for that month.
2. The subscription runs either **indefinitely or for a fixed number of months**, chosen at sign-up.
3. The first month is **billed proportionally from the day of joining** — nobody pays for sessions that had already taken place.
4. **Club days off and cancelled sessions lower the bill on their own.** If a session that was already paid for does not happen, the system records a **refund** — payable in person, or kept as **credit that comes off the next month's bill automatically**.
5. "My trainings" shows the current month's amount and session count, the next month's estimate, any refunds due, and any credit carried forward. Payment is made **upfront, in person with the organizer**.
6. Resigning takes effect **from the following month** — the current one stays active to the end. While the current or a future month is already paid for, resignation is blocked and the client is pointed to the organizer.

The whole cycle notifies itself by email: joining, being added or removed by the organizer, resigning, a change to the slot, the slot being deleted or deactivated from a given date, a single cancelled session, a club day off, a coach's day off, and a fixed-term subscription expiring.

### The 1-on-1 training plan

For clients training individually, the coach marks the account as a **client** — a "Training plan" tile appears in their account.

- **Explicit consent comes first.** Body weight, its trend, calorie targets, effort ratings, and comments are **health data**, so the plan stays locked until the client gives separate, explicit consent to processing it. Without that consent the rest of the account works exactly as before.
- **One shared calendar.** Coach and client see the same entries. A **training** and a **task** (e.g. a calorie target, a stretching session) are always **separate entries** with separate check-offs — a day where the training went well and the diet did not has to be recordable as exactly that.
- **Ticking off belongs to the client**: a training is closed with an effort rating of 1–10 plus an optional note, a task with a simple check.
- **What the coach prescribes, the client cannot rewrite.** Entries created by the coach are read-only on the client's side — what remains is ticking them off and commenting. The client's own entries stay fully theirs.
- **A comment thread under every entry**, with unread markers in both directions, so neither side has to hunt for what changed.
- **An exercise video library and templates.** Materials are attached to an entry from a shared library (the name is filled in from the video's title); a copied or moved training takes its materials with it.
- **Goals across three horizons** — short, medium, and long term — including **weight goals that close themselves** once the 7-day trend reaches the target.
- **Morning body weight with a 7-day trend**, recorded by the client; the coach has a read-only view of the chart.
- **Statistics**: a heatmap, streaks, averages, and attendance — including an overtraining signal that **only the coach sees**.
- **Group sessions appear as a live overlay** on the individual calendar, never as copied entries — so the calendar can never drift away from the monthly bill.

### The organizer's path

The admin panel (hidden, available after logging in) offers tabs to manage everything:

- **Staff** — instructors with a photo, bio, and assignment to categories.
- **Camps / Courses** — types and dates: creation, editing, galleries, ordering, activation. Sign-ups are viewed per date, a registered user can be added by hand, and a bulk email can be sent to everyone on a roster.
- **Trainings** — training types plus everything the recurring slots need, in one tab: the **slots** themselves, the **participants** with their monthly payments (opening a person shows their full training file), **cancelled sessions** with the option to restore them, **club days off**, the **refund register**, and an archive of deleted slots.
- **Clients** — the roster of people training 1-on-1, with unread updates first. Opening a person shows their individual calendar, goals, and statistics; below the roster sit the shared exercise video library and the training templates.
- **Users** — browse and search accounts (paginated), open a member's profile and bookings, send service or marketing emails, and manage administrator roles.
- **Archive** — history of completed dates with their rosters.

Any significant change to a date, a recurring slot, or a session — and any removal of an enrollment — **automatically notifies participants by email**. The communication takes care of itself.

## Key features

- **Account-based online enrollment** with protection against duplicate sign-ups.
- **Monthly training subscriptions** — recurring weekly slots billed proportionally from the day of joining.
- **A bill that keeps itself honest** — club days off and cancelled sessions lower it; refunds are either paid out or carried over as credit against the next month.
- **1-on-1 training plans** — a shared coach/client calendar with tasks, effort ratings, comments, and unread markers.
- **Goals and body weight** — goals across three horizons, plus a morning weight chart with a 7-day trend and weight goals that close themselves.
- **An exercise video library and templates** shared across all clients, attached to individual entries.
- **Transactional emails** — confirmations, change/cancellation notices, and the full recurring-training cycle; **opt-in marketing mailings** with a one-click unsubscribe link.
- **Automatic rules** — enrollment closes 24 hours before the start; past dates are archived; fixed-term subscriptions expire on their own.
- **Staff profiles** with bio and gallery, assigned to the relevant categories.
- **Photo galleries** for session types (with server-side image optimization).
- **Social sharing** (Facebook / WhatsApp / copy link) with rich previews — dedicated Open Graph meta tags served to social-media crawlers.
- **SEO** — a sitemap (`sitemap.xml`) and markup for search engines.
- **GDPR** — privacy consent captured at sign-up, separate explicit consent for the health data of a 1-on-1 plan; deleting an account anonymizes the entire booking history.
- **Dark mode** in the club's visual identity (anthracite / black / orange).
- **Polish-only** — a consistent, single-language interface and communication.

## Security and privacy

- Accounts are **public** — anyone can register and log in (email/password or Google); signing up for a date requires an account, while the admin panel stays protected by an administrator role.
- Authentication is based on **JWT** (a short-lived access token plus refresh), with email verification and password reset.
- Abuse protection: **rate limiting** per IP address across five separate budgets (authentication, account, 1-on-1 plan, admin, public API) and account lockout after a series of failed login attempts.
- The **health data** of a 1-on-1 plan sits behind its own explicit consent — until it is given, the entire client-facing plan is closed, and the rest of the account keeps working.
- The individual-plan area is **invisible to anyone without it**: the server answers as if the feature did not exist, rather than admitting there is something to be denied.
- Participants can exercise the **right to be forgotten** — deleting an account anonymizes the whole booking history in line with GDPR.

## Under the hood (in brief)

The application consists of two parts in a shared repository:

- **Backend** — Java + Spring Boot, PostgreSQL (Flyway migrations), a Spring Security layer, transactional emails, caching, and image optimization. A REST API split into public, user, and administrator zones.
- **Frontend** — React + TypeScript + Vite, Tailwind CSS, React Query, and React Router. A responsive, accessible (a11y) interface with dark mode.

```
fire-academy-backend/    # Java / Spring Boot — API, enrollments, emails, security
fire-academy-frontend/   # React / TypeScript — public site + admin panel
fire-academy-hub/        # Runtime configuration (Docker Compose, env)
```
